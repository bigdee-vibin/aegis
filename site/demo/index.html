Review Center simulation

Simulation - no real data leaves your browser, no real detection engine

This page is not Aegis and does not protect anything. It runs a handful of toy regular expressions on fake sample formats to demonstrate the planned Review Center flow from the design board. A real engine is not built or measured yet, and this toy will miss things. Nothing is sent anywhere, saved or logged; text you type stays in this page and disappears when you close it.

1. Paste text to inspect

Toy patterns: cloud-key-like IDs, sk- style tokens, GitHub-token-like strings, private key headers, JWT-shaped strings (all treated as Critical) and email addresses (Moderate). Please do not paste real secrets or personal data.

What this simulation is not

  • Not the Aegis detection engine. The real design uses deterministic detectors with checksums and format rules, which are not yet verified. These toy patterns are simpler and weaker.
  • Not evidence of effectiveness. When nothing matches here, that says nothing about whether text is safe.
  • Not simulated: the 5-minute approval timeout, hash binding with a cryptographic hash (this page uses a short non-cryptographic checksum for display), network egress control, response inspection, audit logging, coverage tiers.
  • Blocked-critical state: the design gives no approve, edit or override control. This simulation follows that rule.

Design rules come from the design board (see MASTER.md in the repository).

Aegis is pre-release. This simulation makes no network requests after the page files load and stores nothing except your theme choice.
